# Code of conduct

Keelson is maintained by volunteers and used by people protecting real systems. We want everyone who
takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, to be treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people are trying to help, often in a second language.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain why, and suggest what would work better.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that "not now" is a fair answer.
- Respect people's time: search before opening an issue, and give enough detail to act on.
- Keep other people's data out of public threads. Redact account IDs, hostnames, emails and tokens
  from screenshots and logs.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including details of their Cloudflare accounts.
- Disrupting discussions on purpose, or encouraging others to.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block anyone who breaks this code, temporarily
or for good, depending on how serious it is and whether it repeats.

To report something, contact the maintainer privately through the email on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/r4reetik).
Reports are kept confidential. If the report is about the maintainer, contact GitHub through [Report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) instead.

This code applies in every project space, and when someone represents the project in public.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
